Create Better Videos with AI

ClipForge Offline is a desktop video editing application designed to help creators produce short-form content, generate subtitles and prepare videos for social media.

Currently under development. Not available for public download.

Features

AI Video Editing

AI-assisted selection and preparation of video clips.

Automatic Subtitles

Generate and customize subtitles for video content.

Video Export

Prepare videos in formats suitable for social platforms.

Account Management

Manage supported social account connections through official authorization workflows.

TikTok Integration

ClipForge Offline is developing an integration with TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API.

The intended integration allows users to authorize their TikTok accounts using OAuth and upload their own edited videos to TikTok as drafts. Users review and publish their content in TikTok.

TikTok authorization and posting capabilities are subject to platform permissions and approval.

Privacy and Security

ClipForge Offline is designed for local video processing. Social media authorization uses official platform APIs. Users remain in control of their accounts and content.
