ClipForge Offline - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how ClipForge Offline, a Windows desktop video editing application under development, handles information during its use.

2. Local Video Processing

ClipForge Offline is designed to process videos locally on the user's computer. Video files and editing projects remain on the user's device unless the user chooses to use an online integration.

3. TikTok Account Information

When a user connects TikTok, ClipForge Offline requests authorization through TikTok's official OAuth process.

With the user.info.basic permission, the application may access basic account information, such as a display name, avatar, and account identifier, to identify the connected account.

4. Video Uploads

With the video.upload permission, ClipForge Offline may transfer videos selected by the user to TikTok as drafts. Users complete the publication process within TikTok.

5. Authentication and Credentials

ClipForge Offline does not ask users to enter their TikTok passwords directly. Authentication is handled through TikTok's official authorization service. The application is designed to store authorization credentials locally using the operating system's secure credential storage where supported.

6. Third-Party Services

When users authorize integrations or upload videos, relevant information is transmitted to the selected third-party platform. Those services process data according to their own privacy policies.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Users may disconnect supported accounts and remove locally stored authorization information through the application's available account controls. Users may also revoke authorization through the relevant social media platform's account settings.

8. Security

ClipForge Offline aims to limit access to information required for its features. No software or method of data storage can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the application evolves. Changes will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the project developer through: ClipForge Offline GitHub Repository .
